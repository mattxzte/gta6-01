# SOLMAR HEAT II — an open-world crime game for the [Make GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

Dante Cruz is back in Solmar Bay, a sun-soaked coastal city of towers, marinas, ports and beaches. Take eight story jobs, steal any car on the street, outrun the police through roadblocks and helicopter chases, and build a garage of tuned rides — all in the browser, offline, from these static files.

## What is in the game

- **A full open world:** 13 avenues by 11 streets of drivable city — downtown towers, the marina, the port, old town, suburbs and hills, plus a beach and boardwalk.
- **On foot and by car:** third-person walking with jumping and fists, and arcade driving across six car types — sports, sedan, taxi, van, truck and limo — every one of them stealable.
- **Traffic and pedestrians:** cars that follow the roads and obey the lights, citizens who stroll the sidewalks and flee from gunfire.
- **Wanted system:** 0–5 stars. Foot cops, Interceptor cruisers that route through the streets after you, roadblocks at three stars, a police helicopter with a searchlight at four. Lose the heat by hiding, or pay it off at the spray shop.
- **Eight story missions:** taxi fares, a marina car theft, a timed package run, a warehouse shootout, a helicopter getaway, a dusk truck run through a checkpoint, and a finale — stealing the mayor’s limo from the plaza.
- **Combat:** fists, pistol, SMG and scattergun, with tracers, muzzle flashes and enemy shooters who fight back.
- **Progression:** cash from fares and jobs, a gun shop, a diner, Pay ’n Spray, a four-car garage with engine and armor upgrades, and a safehouse that saves the game.
- **Feel and polish:** HUD with a live minimap, day/night cycle with golden-hour dusk, synthesized engine, sirens and gunfire, and four radio stations — all music generated live in WebAudio, no audio files.

## Run it

```sh
npx --yes http-server game -p 8080
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```

Or serve `game/` with any static file server. The game makes no network requests.

## Controls

WASD move / drive · Shift run · Space jump / handbrake · E enter car & interact · F punch · drag mouse for camera · click to shoot · 1/2/3 weapons · Q cycle weapon · M next job · N radio · H horn · C camera · P pause.
