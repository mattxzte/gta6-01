SOLMAR HEAT II

WASD move / drive · Shift run · Space jump / handbrake · E enter car & interact · F punch · Mouse drag camera · Click shoot · 1/2/3 weapons · Q cycle weapon · M next job · N radio · H horn · C camera · P pause

HOW TO PLAY

Dante Cruz is back in Solmar Bay. Take jobs with M, steal cars with E, and watch the stars — the police answer every crime.

On foot: WASD to move, Shift to run, Space to jump, F to punch, click to shoot, 1/2/3 to swap weapons.

Driving: W accelerate, S brake / reverse, A/D steer, Space handbrake, E to get out, H to honk.

The city: gun shop, diner, Pay ’n Spray, Mara’s Garage and the safehouse are marked on the minimap. The garage stores up to 4 cars and sells engine and armor upgrades. The safehouse saves your game.

Wanted: stealing cars, hitting pedestrians and shooting raise your heat. Hide until the stars fade, or pay at the spray shop.

Radio: N changes station. All music is synthesized live — SOL FM, Palma, Neon and Talk Solmar.
